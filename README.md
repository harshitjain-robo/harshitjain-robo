# Harshit Jain

Robotics perception and simulation. MS Mechanical Engineering (Robotics and Control) at Carnegie Mellon, graduating December 2026.

I work on the sensing and physics side of manipulation: segmentation and pose estimation from RGB-D, physics-accurate scene modelling in Isaac Sim and OpenUSD, and getting both to run inside ROS 2 on real hardware.

Currently doing deformable-object manipulation research at CMU's CERLAB.

## Projects

| Repository | What it is |
|---|---|
| [isaac-franka-grasp-lift](https://github.com/harshitjain-robo/isaac-franka-grasp-lift) | A Franka picks up a hanging cable in Isaac Sim. Maximal-coordinate D6 capsule chain, weld against friction grips, and studies of what the physics actually costs |
| [lunabotics-camera-perception](https://github.com/harshitjain-robo/lunabotics-camera-perception) | Camera-only rock perception for CMU's NASA Lunabotics rover. Bootstrapped from prior-year footage, temporal filtering, ground-plane mapping into a ROS 2 costmap |
| [abb-rockbolt-pose](https://github.com/harshitjain-robo/abb-rockbolt-pose) | 6-DoF pose of rock bolts on a mine conveyor, for ABB. Mask R-CNN into point-cloud PCA, plus a study of which half of the rotation was recoverable at all |

Each one ships tests, runs them in CI, and states in its README what it cannot do.

Some of my research is sponsor-confidential and stays in private repositories. Happy to talk through the approach.
